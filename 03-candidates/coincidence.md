# C0 — It is a coincidence

The null. Three small numbers in one potential, no common cause.

**What it needs to be true:** that the probability of the observed near-criticality under a
reasonable prior is not small. This is exactly `02-how-surprising/`'s computation, so this
candidate's standing is decided there and nowhere else.

**Would kill it:** p < 0.01 under every defensible prior tried, for the λ edge alone; or a
mechanism that predicts a second, independent number correctly.

**Would kill its rivals and not it:** a demonstration that each of the three edges has a
different, specific, non-coincidental explanation that does not generalize to the others.
Three separate causes is the coincidence hypothesis with extra steps.

Breaker's standing instruction: keep this alive as long as it deserves to be.
